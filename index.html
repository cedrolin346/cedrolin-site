Cedrolin Capital combines fundamental, long-term equity investing with an actively managed derivatives strategy, built on independent research and rigorous risk control.

The Firm

An owner's mindset, applied with precision.

We approach every investment as a part-owner of a business, grounded in independent analysis of its economics, its management and its price.

Our core holdings are selected through a value-oriented discipline and held over a multi-year horizon. Alongside them, a short-term options strategy is managed under predefined risk limits and strict execution rules.

This allows a long horizon and complete independence of judgment.
