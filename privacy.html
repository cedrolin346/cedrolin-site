1. Scope

This policy explains how Cedrolin Capital handles information in connection with this website and with the internal systems we use to run the firm.

Cedrolin Capital is a private investment firm that manages only its own capital and that of its principals. It is not a registered investment adviser, does not accept capital from outside investors, and does not offer investment services to the public.

2. This website

This website is a static site. It does not set cookies, does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and does not build a profile of visitors. Our hosting provider processes standard server request data — such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested — for the technical operation and security of the site.

3. Email correspondence

If you contact us at the address published on this site, we keep that correspondence and your contact details so we can reply and maintain our records. We do not sell or rent contact details, and we do not use them for marketing.

4. Internal systems and Google services

Our internal tools access only our own company accounts. We do not access, request or hold data belonging to any other person's Google account.

Cedrolin Capital operates internal software that connects to the firm's own Google account — Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts — through Google's published APIs. Access is granted by the owner of that account through Google's standard OAuth consent process, and only for the purposes of running the firm's own administrative workflows, such as maintaining records, correspondence and scheduling.

Information obtained through Google APIs is:

  • used only to operate and improve these internal firm workflows;
  • not sold, rented, or transferred to any third party for advertising, marketing, creditworthiness or any similar purpose;
  • not used to build advertising profiles;
  • accessible only to the firm and to service providers acting on the firm's behalf strictly to operate these tools.

Where automated processing is used, it is carried out by service providers acting on the firm's behalf and solely to operate the tool. Access rights are limited to those needed for the workflow in question, and access can be withdrawn at any time.

5. Disclosure and sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for anyone else's marketing. We disclose information only where it is necessary to operate our systems and services, or where we are required to do so by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Storage and security

Information is stored on infrastructure the firm controls, held with restricted access and protected by access credentials and encryption in transit. Access authorisations such as Google OAuth tokens are stored with restricted permissions and can be revoked at any time.

7. Your choices and revocation

You can revoke access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Since we access only the firm's own account, this applies to the firm's own principal. Requests about correspondence you have sent us can be made to the address below.

8. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our systems or in the law. The date at the top of this page shows the current version.
